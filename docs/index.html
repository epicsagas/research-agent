Paper indexing

🎯

Gap analysis

An LLM maps your topic coverage and surfaces what's missing — unread literature, unanswered questions, methodological holes.

📊

Research reports

Generate literature reviews and state-of-the-field summaries over any topic, saved as Markdown.

📂

Topic trees

Organize research hierarchically with sub-topics, priorities, and per-topic coverage scores.

📖

Reading tracker

Queue, skim, deep-read, and rate papers 1–5. Your reading pipeline, tracked.

Local dashboard

research dashboard opens a live web view of your library, pipeline, history, and reports. Read-only, localhost-only.

How it works

One loop you run for the life of a research question.

STEP 1

Ingest

Pull papers from arXiv, S2, or PDFs into your local index.

STEP 2

Track

Mark what you've read; rate what mattered.

STEP 3

Analyze

Gap analysis finds the holes in your coverage.

STEP 4

Report

Generate a survey — then repeat with sharper queries.

Quick start

Three commands from zero to a survey.

# Install (or: cargo install / brew)
curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -LsSf \
  https://github.com/epicsagas/research-agent/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

# Initialize your workspace
research init

# Build a topic and pull papers
research topics add "Rust async runtime design"
research ingest "async runtime scheduling" --source arxiv --topic <topic-id>

# Find your gaps, then write it up
research gaps --topic <topic-id>
research report --topic <topic-id> --title "Rust Async Survey"
